Modes, maps and Ranked: the plan

Campus Combat, 2026-10-22. What you asked for, what the game already has, what is new, and the order to build it. The mode screen below is our own arrangement (Free Fire was only a reference for the structure).

1 · What you asked for (my understanding)

50 players: battle royale

  • Solo, duo or squad. Press START, the server finds an open session or makes one, random players fill it, then the plane flies and everyone jumps. Last team standing wins.
  • Classic BR on two maps (Varsity Valley, Campus Grounds), just for fun.
  • Ranked BR: the same match, but placement + kills earn rank points.

4 vs 4: team fights

  • Classic 4v4 on several maps (Classic college, Campus Grounds, …), just for fun.
  • Ranked 4v4: your team of 4 presses START, the server finds another team of 4 that chose ranked, and the two fight. The winners rank up; how much depends on kills and damage.
  • Custom Room stays as it is: a Room Card, a code, friends and enemies you invite.

5 · How rank points (RP) move

Ranked 4v4

  • Win +24, loss −16.
  • Performance +0 to +12: kills (2 each, up to 8) and a damage rating (your damage against the match average, up to +4).
  • MVP of the match +4.
  • A loss with a strong game loses less: the performance points are added to −16.
  • Promotion protection: the first loss after a promotion cannot drop the division.

Ranked BR

  • Placement: #1 +30, top 5 +18, top 10 +10, top 25 +2, below that −8.
  • Kills +3 each (cap +18) and damage +1 per 250 (cap +6).
  • Higher tiers pay a "ticket" first (Topper −4, Dean's List −8, Gold Medalist −12), so the top stays earned.
  • Bots give half kill points, and a match with fewer than 2 humans is unrated (the rule the backend already has).

100 RP = one division. Seasons last 8 weeks; at a new season everyone drops back 4 divisions (Senior III → Junior II) and gets a season badge reward. All numbers live in engagement_config, so the admin can tune them without an app update.

Classic 4v4 is the same queue without RP and with no tier limits. If no full team is found in 2 minutes, classic fills the gaps with bots; ranked never does (it widens the search instead).

9 · Order

  1. Rank data and RP rules on the backend, with tests (no screens yet).
  2. Ranked BR first: the session exists; it only needs the mode, the queue split and RP on the result.
  3. The mode grid screen and tier badges in the game.
  4. The team-vs-team queue: Classic 4v4, then Ranked 4v4 on top of it.
  5. Seasons and the admin screens.
