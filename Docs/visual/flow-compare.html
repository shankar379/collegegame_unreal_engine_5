The battle royale flow, step by step

Three.js — 15 in a match
Unreal — 50 in a match
1 · Lobby: pick mode and form the team same
1
SOLO · DUO · SQUADTeam of 1 / 2 / 4. Friends join by invite or squad link; members press READY.
SOLO · DUO · SQUADTeam of 1 / 2 / 4. Party via /v1/party invites; members press READY.
2 · START: find a session or make one different
2
Matchmaking in the browserLeader reserves every member's seat (rooms/{code}/squad), then joins the oldest filling session of that mode + map with room, or opens a new one. Firebase only, no server.
Matchmaking on the serverLeader queues the whole team (POST /v1/br/queue); Postgres places it in the oldest open session with room for all of them, or opens one. A team is never split.
3 · Waiting area: everyone gathers different
3
Island, up to 15 real playersFills for 30 s, plus up to 30 s more for a reserved squadmate still loading. Starts at once if 15 are in.
Platform, up to 8 real playersThe host phone caps humans at 8. Seals at ≥ 2 humans + 12 s, or anyone + 25 s. Never waits for 50 humans.
4 · Fill with bots and count down different
4
Bots fill to 15 · countdown 5 sThe line-up is written once for every game (rooms/{code}/setup), so everyone has the same teams and bots.
Bots fill to 50 · countdown 10 sbs.sealed to every phone. Bots far from humans are "virtual" and only become real when someone comes near.
5 · Plane and jump same (as of today)
5
Plane → jump togetherWhen one squad member jumps, the rest jump too. Chase camera shows the map.
Plane → jump togetherDropWithSquad (added today): one press drops the whole squad, 2.5 m apart.
6 · Match: loot, zone, fight same
6
Bots loot, zone and fall damageBots start with fists, pick up guns, run to the safe zone and take fall and zone damage.
Bots loot, zone and fall damageBots that appear in the first 60 s start empty-handed and loot (TickLoot, added today). Zone and fall damage hit bots too.
7 · Death and spectating different
7
Watches anyone aliveTeammates first, but ◀ ▶ also cycles to enemies (spectate.js).
Teammates onlyCamera moves to the next living teammate; NEXT TEAMMATE button (added today). Never an enemy.
8 · Win and back to the lobby same
8
Last team standing winsResult screen with placement, then back to the lobby; the squad stays together.
Last team standing winsResult screen (CAMPUS KING / #place), BACK TO LOBBY, result posted to /v1/br/:code/result.
